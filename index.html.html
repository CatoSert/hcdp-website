Our Mission

The objective of the Hull Council of Disabled People is to provide a means of consolidation and co-operation amongst both disabled and able-bodied people. We aim to promote the welfare of disabled and vulnerable individuals, advance their education, further their health, and relieve poverty in any way deemed charitable by law.

Our History

The Hull Council of Disabled People (HCDP) is a charitable organisation serving the needs of disabled and vulnerable people in the Hull area. It was conceived during the International year of Disabled people in 1981, and opened its centre at 35 Ferensway in 1983. This multifunctional centre is the base for promoting independent living for people with disabilities in the Hull area.

Services We Offer

Benefits & Welfare Rights

If you require help with filling in forms or enquiring what benefits you are entitled to, our Welfare Rights workers will be happy to help.

To make an appointment please contact us on (01482) 326140.

Cafeteria & Social Centre

Midday meals, teas and light refreshments are provided throughout the day. Our Buffet Service caters for parties by arrangement.

Wheelchair Loan

Wheelchairs are available for hire for just £10 per week OR £2.50 per day. A deposit of £15 is required and this will be paid back to you when you bring the wheelchair back.

We can deliver wheelchairs to you for £3 within a 4 mile radius of our centre.

Zimmer Frame Loan

Zimmer frames are also available for hire for just £3.50 per week. A deposit of £10 is required and this will be paid back to you when you return the Zimmer frame.

Bric-A-Brac

If you have anything you would like to donate to help us raise funds by selling in our Bric a Brac shop, please bring it in or ask if we could collect by phoning or emailing us.

Helpful Support Links

Below are links to partner charities and external support organisations for mental health and disability support:

MIND

A leading mental health charity in England and Wales which works to create a better life for everyone with experience of mental distress.

CMT UK

A major charity which supports people with Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease, a form of Hereditary Motor and Sensory Neuropathy.

MENCAP

A major charity which supports people with Learning Disabilities.

Clothing Solutions for Disabled People

A project which aims to both understand and meet the custom clothing needs of disabled people.

Sports Forum for the Disabled

Dedicated to promoting and developing accessible sporting activities for disabled people.

Sight Support HULL & East Yorkshire (Formerly HERIB)

Helps and supports the changing needs of people with sight loss in the Hull & East Riding area and raises funds to ensure vital support services continue.

REMAP

A national charity which designs and builds custom-made equipment for people with disabilities.

Scope

A UK disability equality charity offering practical advice, information, and support for disabled people and their families.
